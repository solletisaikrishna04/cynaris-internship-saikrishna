Indian Business Performance – KPI Narrative

1. Business Overview

This dashboard analyzes the performance of Indian businesses using revenue, profit, cost, product and regional sales data.

2. Key Performance Findings

* Revenue: Total revenue is ₹36.78 million.
* Profit: Total profit is ₹8.75 million.
* Cost: Total business cost is ₹28.03 million.
* Profit Margin: The business achieved a profit margin of 23.80%.
* Units Sold: Approximately 2,000 units were sold.

3. Trends and Observations

* South shows higher revenue than West in the regional chart.
* Laptops show the highest profit among the displayed products.
* Revenue increased from 2024 to 2025.
* Profitability should be monitored across products and regions.

4. Business Recommendations

1. Focus on products generating higher profits.
2. Investigate opportunities to improve sales in the West region.
3. Monitor business costs to protect profit margins.
4. Analyze yearly revenue changes to support future planning.

5. Conclusion

The KPI dashboard provides an overview of business performance and helps management make informed decisions using data.

The analysis demonstrates how KPI design and Power BI visualization can support business decision-making.
