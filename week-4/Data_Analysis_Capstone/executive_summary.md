# Executive Summary – Indian Business Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

This project analyzes Indian business data to understand business distribution, annual revenue, and differences across states and cities using Python (Pandas) and Power BI.

## 2. Key Findings

- **Total businesses analyzed:** 50
- **Total annual revenue:** ₹3,14,50,000
- **Average annual revenue:** ₹6,29,000
- **Highest revenue state:** Tamil Nadu
- **Tamil Nadu total revenue:** ₹80,50,000
- **City with the most businesses:** All five cities are tied at 10 businesses each, including Tirupati.

## 3. Business Insights

- Tamil Nadu generated the highest total revenue among the five states.
- The dataset contains 50 businesses across five states.
- Each of the five cities has 10 businesses.
- Revenue varies across states.
- Further analysis is needed to understand the relationship between business age and annual revenue.

## 4. Three Actionable Business Recommendations

1. **Revenue improvement:** Study the factors contributing to Tamil Nadu's higher revenue and identify practices that may be useful in other states.

2. **Market development:** Investigate opportunities for business growth across the five cities while considering local market demand.

3. **Business performance:** Monitor annual revenue and business age to identify patterns that can support better business decisions.

## 5. Conclusion

The analysis provides an overview of Indian business distribution and revenue patterns. Python was used for data analysis, and Power BI was used to create an interactive dashboard.
